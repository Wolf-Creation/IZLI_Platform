LEGACY RELATIONSHIP
------------------------------------------------------------

The platform hierarchy becomes:

Legacy
    ↓
Archive
    ↓
Collection
    ↓
Product
    ↓
Legacy Release
    ↓
Product Passport
    ↓
QR Experience
    ↓
Keeper
    ↓
Community
    ↓
Voting
    ↓
Next Legacy Release

Universe is NOT above Legacy.

Universe is an editorial layer attached to Products.

Product contains

- Legacy
- Archive
- Collection
- Universe
- Style Guide
- Recommendation Hub
- Product Passport
- Legacy Releases
