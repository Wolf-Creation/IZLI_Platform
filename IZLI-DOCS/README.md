\# About IZLI



IZLI is not a traditional fashion brand.



IZLI is a \*\*Legacy Platform\*\* that combines Amazigh heritage, premium apparel, storytelling, community participation and digital product experiences into a single ecosystem.



Every product belongs to a Legacy Archive.



Every purchase transforms a customer into a Keeper.



Every Keeper contributes to building future Archives.



The Website, Admin and future Mobile applications are powered by a shared platform called \*\*IZLI OS\*\*.
